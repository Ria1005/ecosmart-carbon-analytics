ABOUT

A student project studying real habits.

EcoSmart analyzes lifestyle survey data — transport, diet, recycling habits, and energy efficiency — to find out which behaviors most affect individual carbon emissions (measured in CO\u2082-equivalent), and turns the findings into a site you can click through rather than a static report.

How it's built

DataCleaned survey data, stored via MongoDB
ModelRandom Forest regression, scikit-learn
AnalysisPandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, SHAP
SiteStatic HTML/CSS/JS, Chart.js, served via Flask

Limitations

This is self-reported survey data from a limited sample, so findings (like diet's smaller-than-expected effect) may not generalize globally. Region/country/city drill-down for transport isn't possible yet — the current dataset has no location field.
