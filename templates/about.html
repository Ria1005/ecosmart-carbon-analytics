ABOUT / ECOSMART

Data-driven sustainability.

EcoSmart is an analytical machine learning project designed to uncover the empirical relationships between daily human habits and their total carbon footprint.

THE DATASET

10,000 empirical records.

All insights and model predictions are generated from a cleaned, real-world dataset of 10,000 lifestyle survey records. The project isolated 10 core behaviors affecting emissions.
