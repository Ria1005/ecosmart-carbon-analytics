FACTOR 03 — RECYCLING

Breadth matters more than the habit itself.

It's not just whether someone recycles — it's how many different materials they recycle. Emissions drop steadily as that count goes up.

Reading this chart

Each point is the average carbon emission for people recycling that many material types (paper, plastic, glass, metal, etc.). The steady downward slope is the signal: breadth, not just participation, is what correlates with lower emissions.
